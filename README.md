# Sygnify

> Seal any agreement on Solana in 30 seconds.

Sygnify is a mobile-first PWA that lets any two people create a permanent, tamper-proof, on-chain record of any agreement — without lawyers, paperwork, or any technical setup from either party.

---

## The Problem

Every day, millions of informal agreements happen — borrowed money, freelance deals, event confirmations, crypto handshakes. All of them live in WhatsApp screenshots or memory. Both are fakeable. Both are forgettable. There is no simple tool that creates a bilateral, cryptographically signed record that neither party can deny later.

---

## How It Works

1. **Person A** opens Sygnify, types the agreement in plain text
2. A **Solana Blink QR** is generated instantly
3. **Person B** scans the QR on any phone browser — no app download needed
4. Person B signs with their **Phantom wallet** — same muscle memory as a UPI scan
5. Both parties receive a **permanent shareable proof link**
6. The record is stored on **Solana forever** — publicly verifiable, immutable

---

## Why Solana

- **Blinks** turn a transaction into a scannable QR — Person B needs zero setup, just a phone
- **Sub-cent fees** make storing thousands of agreements practically free
- This exact UX is impossible to replicate on any other chain at this cost and speed

---

## Use Cases

- Friends splitting money or tracking who owes what
- Freelancers confirming project scope and payment terms
- Crypto community members locking in handshake deals
- Event organisers confirming vendor agreements on the spot
- DAO members ratifying decisions in person
- Any moment two people need proof and currently have nothing but a message

## MVP Features

- [ ] Agreement creation screen — plain text input
- [ ] Solana Blink QR generation from the agreement
- [ ] Second party scans and signs via Phantom — works in any mobile browser
- [ ] Shareable proof link generated for both parties
- [ ] Record stored permanently on Solana
